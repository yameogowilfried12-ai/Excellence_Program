data model & workflow

A single traceable Batch carries dried mango through processing — every stage writes a record against it, every checkpoint gates its progress.

Burkina Faso→Germany · the “ERP of the farm”
01

The core idea

Raw mango arrives from farms and is grouped into batches. Each batch flows through the processing line while QC checkpoints and food-safety controls sit across the whole path.

originFarm
intakeRaw Intake
the threadBatch
stage 1Wash & Sort
stage 2Drying
stage 3Packaging
exitRelease / Ship

QC checkpoints & the food-safety (HACCP) layer span every stage above — they don't sit in the line, they watch over it.

04

Batch workflow

A batch can't advance until it passes the checkpoint or Critical Control Point (CCP) gating each transition. Failures branch to reject or rework.

// batch state machine
Pass → advance Rework → re-enter drying Fail → reject batch
05

Two decisions worth flagging

QC is one table, not six

One QC_CHECKPOINT table with a stage field beats a separate entity per stage. Simpler schema — and “show every check for this batch” comes for free, which is the traceability report the German side will ask for.

Food safety = HACCP mapped onto existing data

Drying (moisture / temperature) and washing (water quality) already are your Critical Control Points. COMPLIANCE_RECORD mostly links evidence and tracks corrective actions — it doesn't re-measure what the stage records already capture.
